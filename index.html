02 — Process

From pitch to launch.

End to end. You never have to find another developer.

  1. 01

    Pitch

    Send us your idea through the form. Rough is fine: a sentence, a sketch, a voice memo. Pitching is free.

  2. 02

    The Call

    We dig into it together and tell you straight whether it'll work, how we'd build it, and what it'd take.

  3. 03

    Build

    You get a written plan with a price that fits the idea. Once you approve it, we design and develop the whole thing.

  4. 04

    Launch

    We ship it, hand over everything, and help you get it in front of real people.

05 — FAQ

Questions.

How much does a call cost?

$5 an hour, paid upfront through Stripe. After you pay, you pick a time and we meet over video.

How much does a build cost?

It depends on the idea. After the call we send a written quote with the scope, price, and deadline. Nothing starts until you approve it.

Who owns the finished project?

You do. If we co-build something with you for a share of what it earns, we agree on that in writing before any work starts.

How long does a project take?

Small builds take days. Bigger games and apps take a few weeks. You'll get a deadline in your quote.

What if my idea is really, really weird?

Good. That's our favorite kind.
