# Business model review — hydrocarbon-forge-website

**Date:** 2026-10-01 · **Trigger:** Nathan's question in #c6-nathan: the process feels rigid,
they don't really ship products, they mostly develop ideas, and monetization seems
to reduce to donations. "Is this a scam business?"

## Diagnosis

The site sells a **build-to-order studio**: *"You bring the weird idea. We forge the
whole thing."* — pitch → call → build → launch, with $5/hr idea calls and
quote-based full builds (index.html hero, #process, #pricing).

But the founders' actual operation is the opposite:

- They are **idea people** — scheming, planning, making gimmicky weird products.
- They do **basic work + a lot of planning**, and don't actually want to deliver
  full builds for other people's ideas.
- They produce a **large volume of their own ideas** they want to advertise.
- The only monetization that fits that profile is **patronage/donations**.

**The core problem: the offer promises a deliverable (full build) the team doesn't
want to deliver.** The gap between promise and deliverable is what makes it feel
scammy — it's a mismatch problem, not an ethics problem. Fix the offer so it matches
what's actually delivered and it's a legitimate business.

**The rigidity** (pitch → call → build → launch) is a generic agency template
pasted onto a studio that mostly makes its own stuff. The template doesn't fit the
product.

Donations are a **support layer, not a business model** — no price anchor, easy to
skip. Fine as fan support, wrong as the core offer.

## Candidate directions

1. **Sell the plan, not the build.**
   Product = a developed, build-ready spec for a weird idea. The planning work they
   already do becomes the paid deliverable: clear scope, clear price, no pretending
   to ship a full game. "We help people develop ideas" *is* the product.

2. **Ship their own gimmicks.**
   The weird products they love making become the actual product — ship them, sell
   them, advertise them. "We forge weird things," not "we build yours."
   The ideas page already leans this way ("Browse by category for inspiration,
   or steal one of these and pitch it as your own").

3. **Donations/patronage** as an optional third layer on top of 1 or 2, not the
   core offer.

## Site changes needed (if direction chosen)

- **Hero:** "You bring the weird idea. We forge the whole thing." → whatever the new
  offer is (e.g. idea-development or shipped weird products).
- **#process:** drop pitch→call→build→launch; replace with the actual flow.
- **#pricing:** $5/hr calls + "depends on the idea" builds → pricing that matches the
  actual deliverable.
- **FAQ:** build/ownership/deadline entries all assume build-to-order; rewrite.
- **ideas.html:** mostly fits already; the "steal one" line works for both directions.
- **Keep:** branding, footer, ideas page structure.

## Open questions (Nathan + Gavin)

- Which direction: sell plans, ship their own stuff, or both?
- Does the "co-build deal" (FAQ: ideas we love may get a co-build) survive?
- Who's the buyer: people with weird ideas, or fans of their weird stuff?
- Answer the "scam" question head-on in the copy if patronage stays in the mix:
  say plainly what people are buying and that donations are optional fan support.

## Not a scam, currently

No fraud: the site over-promises relative to what they'll actually deliver, which is
a fixable positioning error, not dishonesty. Re-align the offer and the concern goes away.
